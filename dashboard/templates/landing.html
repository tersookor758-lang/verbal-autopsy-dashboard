01 — ABOUT

Understanding the story behind the data.

Verbal autopsy is an important public health methodology used to determine the probable cause of death when medical certification or complete clinical records are unavailable.

The Verbal Autopsy Dashboard provides a centralized environment for transforming these records into structured information that can be explored, analysed and reported.
